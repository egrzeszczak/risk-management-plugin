This plugin was created for use with LLMs to facilitate risk management in a project. The risk management process consists of providing answers to the following questions:

* Q1. What am I trying to achieve?
* Q2. What might affect my objectives?
* Q3. Which risks are the most important to me?
* Q4. What should I do about most important ones?
* Q5. Did it work?
* Q6. What has changed?

Skills contained in this plugin attempt to help the user answer each of these questions. Q1 is handled by the `/set-objectives` skill, Q2 is handled by the `/identify-risks` skill, Q3 by `/assess-risks`, Q4 by `/establish-risk-stategies`, Q5 by `/risk-review` and finally Q6 is being handled by `/risk-updates`.

There are additional skills that help the user prepare for the first step, such as establishing the context `/establish-context` and `/set-direction` as helper skills.
